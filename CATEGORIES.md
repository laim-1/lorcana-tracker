## Known approximations in the per-card definitions

- Many per-card abilities pick their choices automatically with a best-for-you heuristic (which card to discard, which item to sacrifice, which location to move to, which reveal to take, "choose one of the following" on Woody/Buzz-style cards), instead of opening a picker for the human player. Targeted "chosen character" effects still use normal tap-targeting, and look-at-top-N effects use the picker.
- "Opponent chooses" effects use a simple AI choice for the opponent (e.g. cheapest character).
- Reveal-hand / look-at-hand effects have no visible reveal UI (no game effect beyond information).
- "Put damage counters" is modelled separately from "deal damage" (ignores Resist, no damage triggers).
