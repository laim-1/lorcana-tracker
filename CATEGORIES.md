## Player choices

Every choice the rules engine makes for a human player opens the on-screen picker (bottom sheet naming the source card): targets for triggered abilities, "you may" (Yes/No), choose-one options, discards (including ones forced by the opponent, which you pick), sacrifices and costs, where to move, up-to-N targets, deck searches, top-of-deck arrangements, recursion from the discard, naming a card, revealed and looked-at hands, and "opponent chooses" effects. The AI picks for itself with the same heuristics as before. tests/engine.test.js scans js/engine.js so any new auto-pick that bypasses the picker fails the build.

Remaining automatic choices (by design or not yet offered):
- Order of simultaneous triggers: they resolve in board order; each one that needs a choice gets its own picker, in that order.
- "You can only ready one character" next turn: the highest-value character is readied.
- Sing Together: the cheapest group of singers that meets the cost is used.
- Damage redirected to a character that takes damage for others goes to the first such character.
- Random effects (random discard, random cards from the inkwell) stay random, as printed.
